# indian-power-sector-stocks
This project presents a comprehensive financial analysis system for selected companies in the Indian power sector using historical stock market data. The primary objective is to evaluate risk, return, and trading opportunities through a data-driven approach.
The study integrates multiple analytical components, including
market risk assessment, credit risk modeling, portfolio analysis, and rule-based trading strategy
implementation.
Historical stock price data from 2020 to 2024 was collected using the yfinance library. The data was
preprocessed to calculate daily returns and ensure consistency. Exploratory analysis was conducted using
visualization techniques such as price trends, normalized comparisons, and correlation heatmaps to understand
stock behavior.
Market risk was evaluated using volatility, maximum drawdown, and rolling volatility, providing insights into
price fluctuations and downside risk. A credit risk scoring model was developed using financial ratios such as
Debt-to-Equity and Return on Equity to classify companies into different risk categories. An equal-weighted
portfolio was constructed to analyze diversification benefits, and performance metrics such as return,
volatility, and Sharpe ratio were calculated.
Additionally, Value at Risk (VaR) was used to quantify potential losses at a 95% confidence level. A moving
average crossover strategy was implemented to generate trading signals and compared with a buy-and-hold
approach. Comparative analysis between individual stocks and the portfolio highlighted the trade-off between
risk and return.
The results demonstrate the effectiveness of combining multiple analytical techniques to support informed
investment decision-making.
